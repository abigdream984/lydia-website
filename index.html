About Me

Hi there! I'm Lydia Jingxian Wang, a student at Santa Sabina College. I believe in pursuing excellence in everything I do, whether it's in the classroom, on the golf course, on stage, or helping my community.

My journey is filled with diverse experiences that have shaped who I am today. From winning golf championships at the state level to performing in musical theatre, from excelling in mathematics competitions to serving my school community, I embrace every opportunity to learn and grow.

I'm passionate about golf, speech & drama, visual arts, music, and making a positive difference in the world around me. Through this website, I invite you to explore my achievements and join me on my exciting journey!
